
A luxurious futuristic apartment floating high above a neon cyberpunk city.
The interior is sleek, sensual, and alive: polished black chrome floors threaded with soft violet pulse-lights that beat like a heartbeat. Walls formed in sweeping curved panels of matte charcoal metal, intersected with glowing neon veins that twist in fluid, organic patterns.

A low bed hovers slightly above the floor with a black-chrome frame, its holographic sheets shifting through violet and rose-gold gradients. A futuristic wardrobe stands open, displaying cyber-leather outfits, neon mesh pieces, and sleek bodysuits on illuminated racks. A bathroom doorway glows softly, with floating screens above a chrome basin.

The chrome vanity displays multiple holographic mirrors arranged in a floating arc. Soft ambience fills the room: faint neon haze, warm diffused lighting, reflections of the city’s colors shimmering across every metallic surface.

Floor-to-ceiling windows reveal Eidolon City outside—towering skyscrapers dripping holographic signage, hover lanes streaking light, neon rain falling sideways across a glowing dusk skyline.

Atmosphere: cinematic, neon-noir, dreamlike, high-tech sensual minimalism.
Style: ultra-detailed, photorealistic, high dynamic range, soft cinematic bloom.
